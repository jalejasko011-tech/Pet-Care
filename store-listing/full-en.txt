Keep the everyday details of your pet's life in one place with Pet Care World.

LOCAL FAMILY VIEW
The local app opens immediately without a code, birth date or account. Optional Family view shows pet names and large play/rest activity buttons; return to Full app with one tap. Family view is a simplified display, not a parental lock. New family-view text is available in English and Serbian, with English fallback in other languages.

Create profiles for dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, pigs and other companion animals. Record vaccinations, treatments, weight, diary notes, food, expenses and important dates.

MEALS AND REMINDERS
Choose your pet's feeding times, record a meal and see how long it has been since your last entry. Optional interval alerts use the schedule you choose. The app records your entries; it cannot detect whether an animal actually ate or recommend a medical diet. Android reminders require notification permission and follow the phone's sound and battery settings.

PRIVATE MEMORIES
Write stories about each pet and keep photos and videos in a private collection on your device. Personalize the app and chat with wallpaper presets, readable text sizes, notification sounds and vibration settings.

YOUR RECORDS STAY WITH YOU
Pet profiles, health entries, Gallery and Memories stay on your device. Create a complete ZIP backup in Settings and restore it by merging records without replacing existing entries. The unencrypted backup includes media within the displayed size limits; keep it in a private, safe location outside app storage.

OPTIONAL PUBLIC CHAT FOR ADULTS
Use the Chat shortcut to join a public text room with a nickname after accepting the rules and confirming you are 18 or older. Messages are visible to participants. Report content, block authors, delete your own messages or delete the device-bound chat identity. Chat needs internet and is not end-to-end encrypted. In this release, chat photo/video uploads and background chat push are not activated.

CLEAR LIMITS
Shopping and veterinarian searches open external services. Community post drafts and family-care lists are local; a shared public feed and cross-device family synchronization are not available. The app has no integrated checkout or paid subscription in this release.

Pet Care World is an organizer and does not replace veterinary care. Contact a veterinarian for illness, urgent symptoms or feeding advice.
