Keep the everyday details of your pet's life in one place with Pet Care World.

LOCAL FAMILY VIEW
The local app opens immediately without a code, birth date or account. Optional Family view shows pet names and large play/rest activity buttons; return to Full app with one tap. Family view is a simplified display, not a parental lock. New family-view text is available in English and Serbian, with English fallback in other languages.

Create profiles for dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, pigs and other companion animals. Record vaccinations, treatments, weight, diary notes, food, expenses and important dates.

MEALS AND REMINDERS
Choose your pet's feeding times, record a meal and see how long it has been since your last entry. Optional interval alerts use the schedule you choose. The app records your entries; it cannot detect whether an animal actually ate or recommend a medical diet. Android reminders require notification permission and follow the phone's sound and battery settings.

PRIVATE MEMORIES
Write stories about each pet and keep photos and videos in a private collection on your device. Personalize the app with wallpaper presets, readable text sizes, notification sounds and vibration settings.

YOUR RECORDS STAY WITH YOU
Private pet profiles, health entries, Gallery and Memories stay on your device unless you explicitly share the basic profile through family care. Create a complete ZIP backup in Settings and restore it by merging records without replacing existing entries. The unencrypted backup includes media within the displayed size limits; keep it in a private, safe location outside app storage.

OPTIONAL SHARED FAMILY CARE
Connect several caregivers to the same pet with a private invitation code. Share a basic pet profile and new meals, walks, treatments and notes in a live diary. The owner manages members and invitations. Codes last up to 24 hours for up to 10 new members. Private histories, photos and local alarms are not automatically synchronized. Sharing requires an adult to connect with a verified email and password. Account recovery uses email; keep the adult password private. Shared data is not included in local backups. New family-sharing text is in English and Serbian, with English fallback in other languages.

CLEAR LIMITS
Shopping and veterinarian searches open external services. Community post drafts and the original family-care lists remain local. A shared public feed is not available. The app has no integrated checkout or paid subscription in this release.

Pet Care World is an organizer and does not replace veterinary care. Contact a veterinarian for illness, urgent symptoms or feeding advice.
