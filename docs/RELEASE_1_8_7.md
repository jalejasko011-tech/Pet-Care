# Pet Care World 1.8.7 — immediate entry

Version code 18. Fresh and upgraded installations open the full local organizer without a PIN, birth date, year or account. Legacy v1/v2 entry settings and age sessions are removed. No entry age is inferred or stored. Optional Family view is a presentation choice, not a protected child account; a single tap returns to the full app. A deliberately selected family view persists across reloads.

View selection does not establish adulthood. Public chat retains its existing explicit rules and 18+ self-declaration before connecting; no chat SDK/auth starts automatically on app launch. Moderation still requires a verified server-assigned role. Media/push/billing remain disabled. Existing pet data and local reminders are retained.

Supersedes entry controls in historical 1.8.4–1.8.6 documents. Physical-phone acceptance, production moderation and Play audience/declaration/submission review remain pending.
