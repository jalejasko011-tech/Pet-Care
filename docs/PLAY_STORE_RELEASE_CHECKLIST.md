## Owner-only gates

1. Open https://play.google.com/console/ using the account that will own the app. Complete real identity/contact verification and Google's registration payment if the account is new. No payment is made by this repository or assistant.
2. Create an app, choose truthful account/category/audience information, set support details and upload the AAB to internal testing. Configure Play App Signing; review the delivered signing certificate and compatibility with existing installations. The direct APK and Play-delivered build may use different signing certificates unless deliberately aligned.
3. Review pre-launch report, target API, permissions, device compatibility and actual installation on physical phones. Check readable screenshots, links and update/data persistence. Do not bypass Play Protect.
4. Complete App access, Ads (none integrated), Target audience, Content rating, Data safety, privacy URL, deletion URL and other required declarations from the actual build. Review the health-app question honestly for pet-only record-keeping; Google determines applicability. Do not invent a rating or certification.
5. Name a human moderator, assign its server-side role, test report/removal/ban/support procedures and monitor the queue. Anonymous bans are identity based and can be evaded by creating a new identity; stronger abuse prevention is a future service decision.
6. If the personal account is covered by the new-account testing requirement, conduct the required closed test before applying for production. Current official guidance requires 12 continuously opted-in testers for 14 days for affected accounts; do not substitute CI or create fake testers.
7. Owner reviews and submits the concrete listing and build. Google decides production access and approval. No public Play release or approval is claimed here.
