# Pet Care World — Privacy Policy (draft for review)
Last updated: 15 October 2026

**Status: draft. Do not submit to Google Play until a private developer contact is added and the final Android build and its SDKs are verified.**

Pet Care World helps users manage pet profiles, photos, vaccinations, health notes, feeding, weight, expenses, diaries and reminders.

## Information handled
Information entered by users (including pet names, photos, medical/vaccination records, weights, costs and reminders) is stored locally on their device using app storage. The current version does not provide user accounts, developer-operated cloud synchronization, or a developer-operated server for those records. The app may access photos selected by the user and may ask for notification permission to schedule local reminders.

## External links
Shopping, offers, veterinary searches and AI features may open external websites or services. These services have their own privacy policies. Do not enter confidential pet or personal information into an external service without reviewing its policy. This version does not contain integrated AI chat, payment processing, ads or affiliate checkout.

## Collection and sharing
Based on the currently reviewed source, the developer does not intentionally transmit pet records to a developer server or sell those records. However, third-party SDK behavior, platform networking and the final Android manifest must be reviewed before any definitive Google Play Data safety declaration. External sites may collect information if opened.

## Storage, retention and deletion
Pet records remain on the device until the user deletes them, clears app storage or uninstalls the app. Uninstalling or clearing storage may permanently remove records. Users should export a backup before removing the app. No cloud backup or account deletion service is currently offered by the developer.

## Security
The app uses local device storage; no promise of end-to-end encryption or cloud backup is made. Device security and backups are the user's responsibility.

## Children
This app is intended for general pet care and is not specifically designed for children. Age-targeting must be confirmed before store submission.

## Changes
This policy will be updated when cloud accounts, advertising, analytics, purchases, AI or other data-handling features are added.
