Pet Care World — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Developer: Jale
Privacy contact: jalejasko011@gmail.com

Information you enter

Pet Care World lets you record pet names, photos, vaccinations, health notes, weight, food, costs, diaries and reminders. The current app stores these records locally on your device. It does not currently offer developer-hosted accounts or cloud synchronization for these records.

Permissions and reminders

You may select pet photos and enable local notifications for reminders. Notification permission is requested for reminder functionality. Your pet records are not intentionally uploaded to a Pet Care World server.

Chat shortcut

The optional global chat is not activated in this release. Opening its shortcut does not contact Firebase or publish data. See chat rules and privacy for the proposed activated service. It must be separately enabled with revised disclosures before live use.

External services

Links for shopping, veterinary searches and AI assistance may open third-party websites, which operate under their own privacy policies. These links are not an integrated payment or AI-chat service. Information you choose to provide to external websites is handled by those websites.

Data retention and deletion

Records stay in local app storage until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling may permanently delete your records. Export your records before doing so if you want a backup. There is currently no Pet Care World account to delete.

Photos, videos and optional contact details

Selected photos and videos are stored in the local gallery (IndexedDB). SOS and community drafts can include a contact number and city you choose to enter. These drafts are private on this device until you select Share. The system share sheet sends only the content you choose to the app or recipient you select. Exported record backups and downloaded media are separate files; delete those files separately if needed. The record export does not include gallery video or photo files.

Network requests and optional catalogs

The app does not automatically send pet records to the developer. Cloud synchronization is disabled in this release. If you configure a supplier catalog URL, the app requests that URL to retrieve products, including on later launches and periodic refreshes. The supplier receives normal connection information such as your IP address. Remove the catalog URL to stop these requests. Searches send the search terms you enter to Google. Websites opened by you can receive connection information and data you provide under their own policies. Web hosting providers may process technical access logs when you use the web version or this public policy.

Advertising and payments

This release has no integrated advertising network, analytics SDK, subscription billing or in-app checkout. Premium and sponsored features are planned; purchases on external sites are handled by those sites.

Deleting local records

Delete individual entries in the app. To remove all app records, gallery media, drafts and settings on Android, open Settings → Apps → Pet Care World → Storage → Clear storage. In a browser, clear this site's stored data. Exported files and copies shared with other services remain there until you remove them separately. Operating-system backup or restore settings may also affect app data; manage those in your device settings. The developer cannot retrieve or delete copies stored only on your device.

Security

Records are stored using local app storage. We do not claim end-to-end encryption or a cloud backup. Protect your device and exported backups.

Children

Pet Care World is a general pet-care utility and is not specifically directed at children.

Changes and questions

We may update this policy if app features or data practices change. Contact jalejasko011@gmail.com for privacy questions.
