Pet Care World — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Developer: Jale
Privacy contact: jalejasko011@gmail.com

Information you enter

Pet Care World lets you record pet names, photos, vaccinations, health notes, weight, food, costs, diaries and reminders. The app stores private records locally on your device. Optional family sharing uploads only the basic pet profile and new shared care entries you deliberately choose to share, as described below. Optional online sharing uses an adult email/password account with verified email. Email addresses are not published to other participants.

Meals and feeding reminders

Meal times and last-fed records stay on your device. You choose the meal schedule and optional interval alert for each pet. The app tracks your entries and cannot detect actual food consumption or prescribe a diet. Native reminders can run outside the app when notification permission is granted; sound follows device volume, silent mode and notification settings.

Permissions and reminders

You may select pet photos and enable local notifications for reminders. Notification permission is requested for reminder functionality. Private pet records are not automatically uploaded. Optional family sharing uses a separate explicit connection and sharing action.

Optional shared family pet care

Online family sharing requires an explicit connection, an adult acknowledgement, a verified email and password entry at each connection. Firebase Authentication stores the account email and authentication information. Email verification is an adult action, not proof of chronological age or a parental relationship. Creating a family sends its chosen name, the basic pet profile (name, species, breed, sex and optional birth date), your chosen family display name, technical member identifiers and timestamps to Cloud Firestore. Joining with a code shares your display name and identifier with that family. New shared meal, walk, medicine/treatment and note entries include their text, author name, technical author identifier and time. Do not enter sensitive human information. These records are visible to current family members and accessible to the project administrator; they are not posted in public chat. Google processes connection information to deliver the service. Data is transmitted using HTTPS; this is not end-to-end encryption.

Invitation codes allow up to 10 new members for up to 24 hours. Share a code privately; anyone with a valid code can join. The server stores its SHA-256 digest, family reference, owner identifier, expiry, use count and last joining identifier/time. Only the owner manages codes and other members. Private existing health history, photos, videos, memories, costs and local alarms are not automatically uploaded or synchronized. Shared content, account credentials and membership links are not included in the local record backup. Blocking a member hides their name and diary entries on this device; it does not prevent them from seeing group content. Reporting an entry or member sends the target identifier, your identifier, a reason and time privately to the family owner. Other members cannot list reports. The owner can delete a reported entry, review/delete reports, remove members and revoke invitations. Removing a member revokes all current invitation codes. If the owner is the problem, block the author, leave and contact the support address. Do not include private human information in reports.

Disconnect stops live updates but does not delete membership or shared entries. A member can delete their own entries before leaving; leaving removes membership but retains past entries. The owner can delete any shared entry or permanently delete the whole family, diary, reports, memberships and invitation records for everyone. Interrupted family deletion can be resumed by the owner on the same device. Deleting a family does not delete the original private pet profile. Account password recovery is available through verified email. The app does not automatically discover families on a new device; retain an invitation or contact support for lost family access. Existing anonymous identities can be upgraded with Create account / preserve identity on the original device. Signing in with a different existing account does not transfer legacy memberships or messages. Ownership transfer is not provided in this version. For lost-access deletion requests, contact the privacy address; ownership must be verified. Copies already made by other members cannot be recalled. Provider logs and operational backups follow Google's retention terms.

Optional global chat

Opening Chat in the full app shows the public-room terms without contacting Firebase. If you choose Join after accepting the rules and confirming that you are 18 or older, an adult email/password account and verified email are required. The adult must enter the password again at each connection. Firebase Authentication processes the email and authentication information; Cloud Firestore stores chat information. Email verification does not prove chronological age. Your chosen nickname, text messages, timestamps and technical author identifier are visible to room participants. Reports include the reporter identifier, message identifier, reason and time and are accessible to the developer/moderator. Google processes connection and technical information under its privacy policy. Pet profiles, health records and gallery files are not automatically shared with chat. No analytics SDK is initialized.

Chat is not end-to-end encrypted. Blocking hides an author's messages on this device; it does not prevent that person from reading the public room. Close or Leave stops the message subscription. Messages remain until their author or moderator deletes them. Reports remain until reviewed and deleted by the moderator. Use the in-chat deletion control to remove your messages and chat profile. This deliberately retains the email account to preserve family access. To delete the email account and related server data, use Request email-account and related-data deletion in Chat or the deletion page; the developer verifies account ownership and handles the request. Account deletion may end family access, so arrange any remaining family care first. Do not send passwords. Contact jalejasko011@gmail.com for moderation or deletion requests if access is lost; ownership must be verified. Copies made by other participants cannot be recalled. See chat rules and privacy and the chat deletion instructions.

Chat attachments

Version 1.8 prepares photo/video attachments but does not enable uploads until the shared media service is configured. When enabled, only attachments deliberately selected and sent in chat are uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage and visible to chat participants. Embedded file metadata may also be included. Loading an attachment is a separate user action. Deleting a message or chat identity also removes its stored attachments. Existing pet gallery content is not automatically uploaded.

Optional chat push notifications

The native notification bridge is bundled but background chat push is disabled in version 1.8.0 until the project owner deploys and activates its backend. It does not register a messaging token automatically. After activation, users must separately opt in and grant notification permission. A private subscription stores the technical messaging token, chosen channel and sound, blocked-author identifiers, language and update time. The server sends a generic new-message alert without message text. Disabling alerts unregisters the token and attempts to remove the subscription. Service providers process technical delivery information. Closing the chat stops its live conversation listener; notification preferences are controlled separately.

Moderation administration

The separate moderator console uses email/password accounts restricted by a server-issued role. Chat reports are private to authorized moderators. Production rules permit private action-history records for that role, including the acting identifier, action, target identifier and server time. These records cannot be edited or deleted through the client. An operational moderator account and retention process still require owner verification.

External services

Links for shopping, veterinary searches and AI assistance may open third-party websites, which operate under their own privacy policies. These links are not an integrated payment or AI-chat service. Information you choose to provide to external websites is handled by those websites.

Data retention and deletion

Records stay in local app storage until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling may permanently delete your records. Export your records before doing so if you want a backup. The optional chat identity and server-side messages require the separate in-chat deletion control described above. Shared-family data requires the family deletion controls below; uninstalling does not delete shared data on the server.

Photos, videos and optional contact details

Selected photos and videos are stored in the local gallery (IndexedDB). SOS and community drafts can include a contact number and city you choose to enter. These drafts are private on this device until you select Share. The system share sheet sends only the content you choose to the app or recipient you select. Exported record backups and downloaded media are separate files; delete those files separately if needed. The record export does not include gallery video or photo files.

Network requests and optional catalogs

The app does not automatically send pet records to the developer. Automatic synchronization of private pet records is disabled. Family sharing contacts Firebase after you choose Connect family care; only explicitly shared content is sent. The optional global chat contacts Firebase only after you choose Join and accept its rules. If you configure a supplier catalog URL, the app requests it only when you explicitly choose Connect supplier or Refresh catalog. A saved URL does not trigger requests on later launches or periodic refreshes. The supplier receives normal connection information such as your IP address. Remove the catalog URL to stop these requests. Searches send the search terms you enter to Google. Websites opened by you can receive connection information and data you provide under their own policies. Web hosting providers may process technical access logs when you use the web version or this public policy.

Advertising and payments

This release has no integrated advertising network, analytics SDK, subscription billing or in-app checkout. Premium and sponsored features are planned; purchases on external sites are handled by those sites.

Deleting local records

Delete individual entries in the app. To remove all app records, gallery media, drafts and settings on Android, open Settings → Apps → Pet Care World → Storage → Clear storage. In a browser, clear this site's stored data. Exported files and copies shared with other services remain there until you remove them separately. Operating-system backup or restore settings may also affect app data; manage those in your device settings. The developer cannot retrieve or delete copies stored only on your device.

Security

Records are stored using local app storage. We do not claim end-to-end encryption or a pet-record cloud backup. Protect your device and exported backups.

Children and entry

Pet Care World opens its local organizer immediately, without a private code, birth date, year or account. The app does not determine a user's age on entry. An optional Family view offers simplified local play/rest activities and hides editing, chat, shopping and sharing until the user chooses Full app. This is a display choice, not a parental lock or verified age control. Existing entry-code and age-session settings are removed on update. No entry birth date is collected or retained. Public chat is for adults and requires explicit rules acceptance, adult acknowledgement, verified email and password entry before connecting. Family sharing also requires an adult to enable each connection with verified email and password. Leaving/closing chat, Disconnect, backgrounding the app or switching to Family view stops the online session; reopening the local Full app does not unlock online access. Keep adult passwords away from children. The app does not verify identity or parental relationship. No ads or in-app payments are active.

Changes and questions

We may update this policy if app features or data practices change. Contact jalejasko011@gmail.com for privacy questions.

Private memories and appearance settings / Privatne uspomene i izgled

Memories (stories, photos and videos) and wallpaper, text-size and notification preferences are saved on your device. Memories are never automatically sent to public chat or Firebase. Anyone with access to your unlocked device may access local memories. Uninstalling or clearing application data can erase them. The separate Memories backup includes its media and is not encrypted; store it privately. Standard pet-record export does not include Gallery or Memories media. Gallery media are not included in the Memories backup.

Uspomene (priče, slike i snimci) i podešavanja izgleda i obaveštenja čuvaju se na uređaju. Ne objavljuju se automatski u chatu. Pre brisanja aplikacije sačuvajte posebnu kopiju uspomena sa prilozima. Kopija nije šifrovana; čuvajte je privatno.

Complete device backup / Potpuna kopija uređaja

Version 1.8.0 adds an unencrypted ZIP containing pet records, Gallery media, Memories, private post drafts, language and appearance/reminder preferences. It excludes shared-family records, family identity/membership links, public chat messages, chat identity, push tokens, moderator credentials and supplier connection settings. Files stay on your device until you deliberately save or share them. Restore merges new records, skips matching identifiers and uses a local recovery journal to undo an interrupted restore. The app temporarily writes export files to its cache for Android sharing; the system can clear this cache. Remove exported copies separately.

Potpuna ZIP kopija obuhvata evidenciju, Galeriju, Uspomene, privatne nacrte i podešavanja. Ne sadrži chat identitet, javne poruke, tokene niti lozinke. Kopija nije šifrovana. Sami birate gde je čuvate ili šaljete. Vraćanje dodaje nove podatke i preskače duplikate.
