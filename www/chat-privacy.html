Global chat / Globalni chat

Updated: 23 October 2026

Global text chat is optional. The Chat shortcut opens the terms. Firebase is contacted only after you accept the rules and choose Join.

When you join

Joining requires explicit acceptance of these rules. Participants must be 18 or older. A verified adult email/password Firebase account is required, with password entry at each connection. The email is not public. Verification is an adult action, not proof of chronological age or parental relationship. Keep the password away from children. Your chosen nickname, text messages, message time and technical author identifier are visible to chat participants. Pet records, photos, email addresses and your legal name are not automatically published. Do not post private addresses, phone numbers, document numbers or other people's personal information. Do not post harassment, threats, abuse, illegal content or spam. Photo/video uploads are prepared but disabled in version 1.6 until shared media storage is configured. When enabled, deliberately sent attachments (including embedded metadata) are visible to chat participants, stored in Firebase Cloud Storage and removed with their messages or identity. Images are limited to 10 MB and videos to 25 MB. Media loads only when a participant chooses Load attachment.

Storage and security

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore provide the chat identity and shared message storage. Google may process connection and technical information, including IP addresses, according to its service terms and privacy policy. Chat messages are not end-to-end encrypted. A human moderator can review reports, remove messages and restrict identities. Blocking hides a participant's messages on this device; it does not prevent them from reading the public room.

Automatic text moderation — activation pending

A prepared server filter checks text and nicknames for SR/EN abuse patterns, spam, links, contact details and selected formatting/script risks before publication. It is not an AI classifier and cannot detect all harmful content or languages. It is not active until the backend and matching rules are deployed. When activated, flagged messages remain private to their author and moderators until reviewed. Ordinary messages may be published automatically. Users can view/delete their review submissions and contact support to appeal. No automatic permanent ban is made. Submission copies remain until deleted by the author or through an account-deletion request; rate-limit records retain the last submission time/hash and current hourly count. Moderation audit records are retained for abuse handling, with ownership-verified deletion requests reviewed by the developer. No text is sent to a separate AI provider.

Automatski filter teksta je pripremljen, ali čeka aktivaciju servera i pravila. Proverava obrasce na srpskom/engleskom i ne otkriva sav štetan sadržaj. Zadržana poruka nije javna; vide je autor i moderator do odluke. Pregled i brisanje svojih zadržanih poruka su u chatu, a žalbe se šalju podršci. Nema automatske trajne zabrane. Kopije predatih poruka ostaju do brisanja od autora ili obrade zahteva za brisanje naloga. Evidencija ograničenja slanja sadrži vreme, sažetak poslednje poruke i broj slanja u satu. Evidencija moderacije služi obradi zloupotreba. Nema slanja posebnom AI provajderu.

Deletion and retention

Messages remain until their author or moderator deletes them. Use Delete beside your own message or Delete chat profile and my messages to remove your public messages and chat profile. The email account is retained when deleting only the chat profile to preserve family access. Account password recovery uses email. Request deletion of the account and associated server data through the deletion page; account ownership must be verified. For deletion requests after losing access, contact jalejasko011@gmail.com with message details; ownership must be verified. Moderation reports remain until reviewed and deleted by the moderator. Copies made by other participants cannot be recalled. Firebase's operational backups and technical logs follow the provider's retention terms. Leaving or closing chat stops the message subscription, but does not delete messages or identity.
