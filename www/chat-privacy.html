Global chat / Globalni chat

Updated: 16 October 2026

Global text chat is optional. The Chat shortcut opens the terms. Firebase is contacted only after you accept the rules and choose Join.

When you join

Joining requires explicit acceptance of these rules. Participants must be 18 or older. A device-based anonymous Firebase Authentication identity is created. Your chosen nickname, text messages, message time and technical author identifier are visible to chat participants. Pet records, photos, email addresses and your legal name are not automatically published. Do not post private addresses, phone numbers, document numbers or other people's personal information. Do not post harassment, threats, abuse, illegal content or spam. Photo/video uploads are prepared but disabled in version 1.6 until shared media storage is configured. When enabled, deliberately sent attachments (including embedded metadata) are visible to chat participants, stored in Firebase Cloud Storage and removed with their messages or identity. Images are limited to 10 MB and videos to 25 MB. Media loads only when a participant chooses Load attachment.

Storage and security

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore provide the chat identity and shared message storage. Google may process connection and technical information, including IP addresses, according to its service terms and privacy policy. Chat messages are not end-to-end encrypted. A human moderator can review reports, remove messages and restrict identities. Blocking hides a participant's messages on this device; it does not prevent them from reading the public room.

Deletion and retention

Messages remain until their author or moderator deletes them. Use Delete beside your own message or Delete chat identity and my messages to remove all your messages and chat identity before uninstalling or clearing app data. Your anonymous identity cannot be recovered on another device. For deletion requests after losing access, contact jalejasko011@gmail.com with message details; ownership must be verified. Moderation reports remain until reviewed and deleted by the moderator. Copies made by other participants cannot be recalled. Firebase's operational backups and technical logs follow the provider's retention terms. Leaving or closing chat stops the message subscription, but does not delete messages or identity.

Pravila i privatnost na srpskom

Globalni tekstualni chat je opcioni. Ulazak zahteva prihvatanje pravila i punoletstvo (18+). Nadimak, poruke, vreme i tehnički identifikator vidljivi su učesnicima. Podaci o ljubimcima se ne objavljuju. Zabranjeni su privatni podaci, uvrede, pretnje, spam i zlostavljanje. Prijavite poruku ili blokirajte autora. Blokiranje sakriva poruke samo na vašem uređaju. Moderator obrađuje prijave, uklanja poruke i ograničava identitete. Poruke nisu end-to-end šifrovane. Svoje poruke i identitet obrišite u chatu pre brisanja aplikacije; anonimni identitet se ne može vratiti na drugom uređaju. Prijave ostaju do obrade i brisanja od strane moderatora.
